# Someday - board swimlanes

Horizontal swimlanes that cut across every column (group by area or assignee). Parked until after 1.0.

`priority: someday` isn't a known scale, so it renders as a neutral, muted chip and sorts below every recognised priority — but still above a card carrying no priority at all. It is in the board note's `priorities` list, so it stays on offer even if this card goes away.
