# Someday - board swimlanes

Horizontal swimlanes that cut across every column (group by area or assignee). Parked until after 1.0.

`priority: someday` is not a word the plugin knows, so its colour comes from the board note's own `priorities` list: it is last on that list, so it is drawn in the calmest tone of the ramp rather than as a neutral grey chip, and it sorts below every recognised priority — but still above a card carrying no priority at all. Being on that list is also why it stays on offer even if this card goes away. For a board whose scale is custom from top to bottom, see `custom-scale/`.
