# The detail panel's title has no answer for a title wider than the header

## What is asked

Rafa asked on 2026-08-27 to test and settle how the detail panel's title behaves when the title in use is too big for the space available.

## Current behaviour

The header is a flex row (`.folia-detail-header`, `src/styles.css`) with the title as a `flex: 1 1 auto` `<h2>` (`.folia-detail-title`) beside the action buttons. There is no rule for overflow: no `overflow`, `text-overflow`, `word-break`, or line clamp. A long title wraps onto as many lines as it needs, so the sticky header grows and pushes the body down; a single unbroken token (a long slug-style file name, a URL used as a title) does not wrap at all and can push the action buttons out of view or widen the panel. The behaviour has not been checked in the three panel modes (modal, side, and the narrow side panel after resizing) nor with the header being `position: sticky`.

## How to reproduce

Give a card a `title:` override of a few hundred characters, and separately one made of a single long word without spaces (or name the file that way). Open it in each panel mode, and resize the side panel to its minimum width. Observe the header height, whether the close/done/open/delete buttons stay reachable, and whether the sticky header hides a meaningful part of the body while scrolling.

## Expected outcome

A defined, tested behaviour for both the wrapping and the unbreakable case in every panel mode: the actions stay reachable, the panel does not grow wider than its mode allows, and the full title remains recoverable (a tooltip, a wrap, or a clamp that expands). Add a test that pins whichever behaviour is chosen so it does not regress. If 20260827.01 moves the title out of the header and into a field, this entry applies to that field's rendering and to whatever remains in the header.

## Status (2026-08-28)

Implemented and merged on `main`. `92f61b6` (*fix(detail): give the panel an answer for a title wider than it is*) settled both cases: the header `<h2>` (`.folia-detail-title`) clamps to two lines with `overflow-wrap: anywhere` and carries the full title as its `title` tooltip, the action buttons are pinned `flex: 0 0 auto` so they keep their width whatever the title does, and the complete title lives in the **Resulting display title** row below, clamped to three lines with a click-to-expand button. `b756a18` is what put that row there in the first place.

Automated coverage lives in `test/ui.test.tsx` (the long-title and unbreakable-token cases) and `test/visual-regression.test.tsx` with its snapshot in `test/__snapshots__/visual-regression.test.tsx.snap`.

**Live verification in Obsidian has not been done.** Line clamping, sticky-header behaviour and the side panel's real minimum width are exactly the things jsdom cannot answer.
