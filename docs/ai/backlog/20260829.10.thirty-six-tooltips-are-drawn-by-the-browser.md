# Thirty-six tooltips in the board are drawn by the browser, not by Obsidian

## What is asked

Finding 01-05 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 01](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/01.ui-components-and-primitives.md), cross-referenced as 06-15) counted 36 `title` attributes across `src/ui/*.tsx` — 24 literals and 12 JSX expressions — where Obsidian exposes `setTooltip` and draws its own tooltip element.

## Current behaviour

A `title` attribute is drawn by the operating system, after the browser's own delay, in the browser's own chrome, with no theme, no placement control and no place in the app's layer stack. The counts by file are in the finding (20 in `CardDetail.tsx`, 9 in `CardItem.tsx`, 2 each in `Column.tsx` and `CardContextMenu.tsx`, one each in three more).

Two facts from the live pass against Obsidian 1.13.7 shape this. The app's tooltip is a `div.tooltip` appended to the body at `z-index: 70`, and it is driven by `aria-label`, not by `title`: hovering a Folia element carrying only `aria-label` produced it, hovering one carrying only `title` did not. So the 93 rendered board elements that carry both already show Obsidian's tooltip, and their `title` is redundant rather than load-bearing. What remains unverified is what a `title` does on a phone at all.

`setTooltip` takes an `HTMLElement`, and `src/ui/**` may not import `obsidian`, so every site becomes a ref plus an effect reaching a `CardRepository` port method — one small hook, but 36 call sites behind it.

## How to observe

Hover any control in the board that carries a tooltip and compare what appears against the tooltip on a control in Obsidian's own interface next to it: different delay, different look, and it does not sit in the app's layer order. Then check the same element for a duplicate `aria-label`, which is where several of the 36 are simply redundant.

## Expected outcome

One consistent tooltip in the plugin, or a written decision that the browser's is acceptable and why — and, either way, the redundant `title` attributes on elements that already carry the same text as an `aria-label` are removed rather than left as a second source of truth.
