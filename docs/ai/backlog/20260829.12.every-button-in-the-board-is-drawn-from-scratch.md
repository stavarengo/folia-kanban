# Every button in the board is drawn from scratch, and the specificity fight that follows is now a documented hazard

## What is asked

Findings 01-08 and 02-30 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 01](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/01.ui-components-and-primitives.md) and [section 02](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/02.styling-theme-and-design-language.md)) read the same decision from two sides: the component choice, and the stylesheet that follows from it.

## Current behaviour

Thirteen button classes are listed together at `src/styles.css:117-137`, each named twice so the rule out-specifies Obsidian's own `button:not(.clickable-icon)`, purely to reset the theme's raised button face. `.folia-btn`, `.folia-btn-primary`, `.folia-btn-danger` and `.folia-icon-btn` (`1173-1236`) then rebuild padding, radius, background and colour from `--folia-*` tokens. On the component side, nothing in the plugin goes through `ButtonComponent`; the three shapes that have an exact native equivalent (primary, danger, icon-only) are drawn by hand alongside ten that are Folia's own vocabulary and have no equivalent.

The doubling convention is enforced by `scripts/check-button-styles.mjs`, which also refuses any `<button>` in `src/ui/**` carrying no `folia-*` class — so "drop the class and let the theme dress it" fails the guard as written today. Two live consequences of the same convention are already tracked: 20260829.01 (a doubled rule out-ranking the plugin's own single-class rules, with three measured examples) and 20260829.02 (a `.folia-link` inheriting the theme's `white-space: nowrap` and fixed height and overflowing its panel). This entry is the cause those two are symptoms of.

The findings are explicit that a wholesale swap is not the answer: a kanban board legitimately wants denser controls than the app's settings buttons, and `mod-cta` cannot express a chip or a 26px icon button. What they identify as recoverable without touching the design is the radius, the interactive background variables and the three shapes that already exist natively.

## How to observe

Read `src/styles.css:117-137` and count how many rules exist only to undo the theme, then compare a Folia primary button against a `mod-cta` button in Obsidian's own settings on the same theme. `pnpm check:button-styles` shows what the guard currently requires.

## Expected outcome

An explicit boundary between the buttons that are Folia's own vocabulary and the ones that are Obsidian's control wearing a different coat, with whatever the guard needs to allow the second group written into it. Closing this is what makes 20260829.01 and 20260829.02 finite rather than recurring.
