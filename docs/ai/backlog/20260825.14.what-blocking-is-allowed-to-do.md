# What a blocking relationship is allowed to do to the board

## What is asked

Blocking currently only makes itself visible: a *Blocked* / *Blocks n* marker on the tile, and the two lists in the detail panel. It changes nothing else. Two consequences were raised and deliberately left undecided when it shipped.

**Dragging.** Rafa asked whether a relationship type could carry implications, blocking being the obvious case: should a blocked card be allowed to move into a given column? The first version says yes to everything — moving a blocked card is not refused, not warned about, not even nudged. That is consistent with the board's stated posture (the README says it "nudges (never blocks)", and soft WIP limits are purely visual), but it was chosen for consistency rather than because anyone decided it is what the feature should do. His constraint, in his words: the plugin should not "enforce any particular usage or workflow. Everything should remain up to the user."

**Searching.** The filter grammar (`parseFilter` / `matchCard` in `src/ui/cardView.ts`) has tokens for status, priority, area, tag, due and context, but none for blocking. There is no way to ask the board "show me everything that is not blocked", which is the question the marker makes people want to ask.

## How to observe it

In the `examples/feature-showcase` vault, `Draft the announcement post` shows a *Blocked* marker. Drag it into **Done**: it goes, with no reaction of any kind. Then press `/` and try to filter for it — no token matches on the relationship, so the only way to find blocked cards is to look at every tile.

## Expected outcome

A decision, recorded, on whether blocking stays purely informational or gains consequences, and if it gains them, what a consequence may look like in a plugin whose only existing rule nudges rather than refuses. The search side is the smaller, more clearly wanted half: a way to filter the board by whether a card is blocked or blocking.
