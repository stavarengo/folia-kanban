# Obsidian's own tooltip paints over an open Folia menu, because both ladders share one numeric space

## What happens

Finding 02-25 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 02](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/02.styling-theme-and-design-language.md)), confirmed in the running Obsidian 1.13.7 during the report's live pass.

`src/styles.css:99-108` declares Folia's z-index ladder — base 1, raised 2, sticky 30, toast 50, scrim 55, menu 60, popover 70, modal 80, top 90 — and applies it to surfaces that are portalled out of the board and into the document body: the card context menu (80), the column popover (60), the detail panel float (55) and the modal backdrop (90). Obsidian's own `--layer-*` scale occupies the same numbers, with five exact collisions. The observed consequence: Obsidian's tooltip sits at layer 70 and paints over an open Folia menu at 60.

Because the two ladders were never compared, the stacking order between a Folia surface and an app surface is a coincidence rather than a decision, and it can change on either side. `scripts/check-tokens.mjs` asserts the ladder against `tokens/source/zindex.tokens.json`, which is the plugin agreeing with itself; nothing compares it to the app.

## How to reproduce

Open the column popover on a board and hover a control that carries an Obsidian tooltip while the popover is open: the tooltip renders on top of it.

## Expected outcome

The surfaces Folia portals out of its own root sit in the app's stacking order on purpose. The in-board rungs (base, raised, sticky) have no Obsidian equivalent and the finding is explicit that they should stay as they are.
