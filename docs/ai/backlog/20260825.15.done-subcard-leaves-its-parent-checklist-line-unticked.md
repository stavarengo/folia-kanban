# A subcard reaching Done leaves its line in the parent's checklist unticked

## What happens

A card's `## Subtasks` checklist holds two kinds of line, and the two now disagree about what finishing means.

An inline todo has one line and one place to say it is done, so the plugin keeps its checkbox and its `[status:: …]` column in step: dragging the tile into Done writes `- [x]`, and ticking the box moves the claim to Done (`syncSubtaskClaim` and `moveSubtask` in `src/model/board.ts`).

A subcard is a file, and its two halves live in two notes. Its column is the child note's own `status` frontmatter; the line that names it in the parent, `- [ ] [[Child]]`, has a checkbox of its own that nothing writes. So moving the child card to Done — by dragging its tile, by the card menu's "Mark done", or by editing the child note — leaves the parent's line unticked. The parent's progress bar counts every checklist line by its own checkbox (`cardStats` in `src/model/card.ts`), so it goes on reporting finished work as outstanding, and `nextTodos` keeps offering it as a next action.

## How to reproduce

On the showcase board, open `Plan the v1.0 launch` (in Todo). Its progress reads `1/4`. Drag the `Write the changelog` tile from Next Up into Done. The child note's `status` becomes `done` and its tile sits in Done, but the parent still reads `1/4` and its checklist still shows `- [ ] [[Write the changelog]]`.

## Why it matters

The board's own claim is that a subitem behaves the same whichever kind it is. This is the one place where it does not: the same gesture on the two kinds of subitem leaves the parent card telling two different stories about how much of its work is left.

## The risk in writing it, which is why it was left open

The obvious fix — tick the parent's line whenever a child reaches Done — was judged too risky to add alongside the change that introduced placement, and the reason is worth carrying forward rather than rediscovering.

A checklist line is addressed by `SubItem.index`, its position among the checkbox lines of the `## Subtasks` section. The board knows a child's parent (`parentOf`) and the parent's `childLinks` in order, but `childLinks` counts only the linked lines while `index` counts every checklist line, so the two are not the same number. Deriving one from the other means re-reading the parent's body and matching the link, which is a second file read and a second write on a path that today is one write to one note. Getting the index wrong ticks somebody else's todo, silently, in their own note.

The reverse direction is a design question, not just a mechanical one: if someone unticks the parent's line, does the child card come back out of Done? Whatever answer this takes, it should be the same answer inline todos already give.

## Expected outcome

A subcard reaching the done column and an inline todo reaching it leave the parent card saying the same thing about its own progress, and no write can land on the wrong checklist line.

## Likely code areas

- `src/model/board.ts` — `moveCard`, `syncSubtaskClaim`, `parentOf` / `childLinks`
- `src/model/card.ts` — `parseSubtasks`, `setSubtaskDone`, `cardStats`
- `src/obsidian/vaultRepo.ts` — `applyMove`
