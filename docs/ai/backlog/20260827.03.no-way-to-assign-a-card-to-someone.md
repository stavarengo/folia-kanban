# No way to assign a card to someone, not even to yourself

## What is asked

Rafa reported this on 2026-08-27: he opened a card intending to assign it to himself and found no option to do so anywhere in the UI.

## Current behaviour

The plugin has no notion of an assignee. A search of `src/` for assignment vocabulary finds nothing beyond `reassignColumn` (moving a column's cards to a neighbour when the column is deleted). The card detail panel offers status, priority, due, order, type, the relationship keys, and free-form properties; the card tile and the board filters know nothing about who owns a card. The only identity the plugin holds is the "Your name" setting (`userName`), used solely to sign comments and to decide what counts as unread.

## How to observe

Open any card's detail panel and look for a way to name a person as responsible; try the card's context menu on the board; try the board filter. None offers it.

## Why it matters

"Who is working on this?" is a question a kanban board answers by design, and it is the first thing a shared board needs. Even for a single-user vault, "mine" is a natural filter once the board carries cards created by agents, scripts or other tools.

## Expected outcome

A card can be assigned to a person from the UI, "myself" being the obvious one-click case (which presumes the plugin knows who "I" am, hence the link to the "Your name" entry). The assignment is visible on the card where it is useful (tile, detail panel) and usable as a filter. Since cards are plain notes, the assignment should live in the note's frontmatter the same way status and priority do, so it stays editable by hand and by other tools.
