# The settings tab is invisible to Obsidian's settings search

## What is asked

Obsidian 1.13.0 added a declarative settings API: a `PluginSettingTab` describes its settings by returning them from `getSettingDefinitions()`, and Obsidian indexes those descriptions so the settings appear in the app's global settings search. `KanbanSettingTab` (in `src/main.ts`) still builds its UI imperatively in `display()`, so none of its eleven settings can be found that way.

## Current behaviour

A user on Obsidian 1.13.0 or later who searches the settings dialog for "board pan", "your name" or any other Folia Kanban setting gets no result; the settings are reachable only by navigating to the plugin's own tab.

This is also the one finding that Obsidian's community-directory scan still reports on the plugin. The local reproduction of that scan, `pnpm obsidian-scan:check`, prints it as `baselined` and points back at this entry:

```text
baselined  src/main.ts:365:7  obsidianmd/settings-tab/prefer-setting-definitions
```

## Why it was postponed

The plugin's `manifest.json` declares `minAppVersion: 1.7.4`, and `obsidian.d.ts` states that `display()` is **not** called once `getSettingDefinitions()` returns a non-empty array. Adopting the API today therefore means either maintaining two parallel settings UIs for the 1.7.4–1.12 window, or losing the settings entirely for everyone below 1.13. Neither is worth trading for one advisory scan warning.

## Expected outcome

When the plugin's `minAppVersion` reaches 1.13.0 — or when a version-guarded path is judged worth its cost — the settings tab exposes its settings through `getSettingDefinitions()` (with `getControlValue`/`setControlValue` for persistence), every setting is findable from Obsidian's settings search, and the baseline entry is removed from `scripts/obsidian-scan.mjs` so the scan check goes back to failing on any finding at all.
