# The raw-value audit does not detect most of what it is documented to police, and a waiver was retired on that basis

## What happens

Finding 02-10 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 02](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/02.styling-theme-and-design-language.md)).

`scripts/audit-raw-values.mjs:52-72` is the whole detector list: nine regular expressions covering hex colours, `rgb()`/`hsl()`, box shadows, font families, z-index, px lengths, font weights, bare opacity and half-pixel lengths. The spacing detector is `/\b\d{2,}px\b/` — two or more digits — so every single-digit px value in the stylesheet passes unseen. There are 164 of them in layout declarations alone. There is no detector for `color-mix()`, for line heights, or for numeric size props in TSX (which is why the 34 icon sizes in the icon-size entry are invisible to it), and `--folia-*` declaration lines are exempted wholesale at line 74, which is where the nine hard-coded theme fallbacks of the colour entry hide.

The governance half is the reason this is an entry rather than a note: `tracking/waivers/0001-raw-value-debt.md` was retired on the grounds that nothing remained to migrate, and that judgement was made against this coverage. The waiver also cites a stale line number.

## How to observe

Run `pnpm audit:raw-values` and read `scripts/audit-raw-values.mjs:52-72` beside `src/styles.css`: grep the stylesheet for `\b\d px` single-digit lengths and for `color-mix(` and compare the counts against what the audit reports.

## Expected outcome

The audit's coverage and the claims made about it agree — either the detectors reach what the waiver says they reach, or the waiver and the documentation say plainly what is not covered. The finding warns that widening the detectors produces a large first baseline, so part of the answer is deciding which of those categories has a token home worth having. The related entry 20260828.01 is about the same script from the other side, the count that only ever grows.
