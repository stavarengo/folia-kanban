# Date handling is written by hand beside the Moment the app already bundles

## What is asked

Findings 03-07 and 03-08 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 03](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/03.editor-rendering-search-and-dates.md)). Both are small, and the report reads them as conditional rather than urgent; they are recorded together so that the condition is noticed when it arrives rather than rediscovered.

## Current behaviour

`dayDelta` (`src/ui/cardView.ts:221`) parses two `YYYY-MM-DD` values with `Date.parse` at local midnight and divides the millisecond difference by one day; `dueInfo` (`:236`) turns that into "Yesterday", "Today", "Tomorrow", `in Nd` and `Nd ago`. `dateOnly` and `stamp` (`src/model/dates.ts:7` and `:11`) assemble `YYYY-MM-DD` and `YYYY-MM-DD HH:mm` from `Date` getters with hand-written zero padding. Obsidian bundles Moment and exposes it, and the calls that would replace the arithmetic were checked against the installed typings in the finding.

The report's reasoning for leaving both alone today: the functions are short, the labels are necessarily Folia's own, and adding a formatting abstraction for two of them would be harder to read than what is there. What would change the answer is named: due dates accepting more input forms, any configurable calendar behaviour, or configurable date formats.

Related and deliberately not part of this: the unread-comment ordering's timezone-free timestamps are a written decision (`docs/decisions.md`, "Unread-comment ordering assumes one clock") and stay as they are.

## How to observe

Read the four functions above; they are a few lines each.

## Expected outcome

Nothing today. This entry is the trigger: when due dates gain another accepted form or the plugin gains a configurable date format, the hand-written arithmetic is replaced rather than extended.
