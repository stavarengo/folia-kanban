# The detail panel should name the file, name the override, and explain the title that results

## What is asked

Rafa asked for this on 2026-08-27 while looking at the card detail panel (`src/ui/CardDetail.tsx`).

## Current behaviour

The panel shows the resolved title as its header (`<h2 className="folia-detail-title">{card.title}</h2>`) and offers one editable field, the property row labelled "Display title" (`DisplayTitleRow`), which writes the card's `title:` frontmatter key. The file name is not a field anywhere in the panel; it only appears indirectly, as the placeholder of the "Display title" row when the fallback happens to be the basename. Nothing in the panel says which of the three sources (`title:` key, heading, file name) produced the header, nor why; that knowledge lives only in `resolveTitle` in `src/model/cardTitle.ts` and in `Card.titleSource`.

## Expected outcome

1. The title moves out of the header into the panel body as a field labelled **File Name**, holding the card's basename (the identity that `[[wikilinks]]` bind to). Editing it is a rename, which the repository already supports through `renameCard` (link-aware).
2. Right after it sits the field that today is "Display title", relabelled **Override card title**, still writing the `title:` key.
3. Under those two fields, a secondary line labelled **Resulting display title** shows the title the card would get from the values currently typed in the two fields, live, whether or not they have been committed. It must be computed by the same `resolveTitle` the board uses, under the board's current title mode, so the preview can never disagree with the tile.
4. Next to that line (or at the end of the fields, whichever looks better), a link as short as a word or two, in the spirit of "Explain title" / "Why this?", that opens a tooltip or small popup showing the decision flow that produced the title, with the branch that was actually taken highlighted.

The constraint that matters for point 4: the picture of the flow must not be a second copy of the algorithm that can drift from it. `resolveTitle` should be able to return, alongside its result, metadata describing the path it took (which sources were consulted, in what order, what each one answered, and why the winner won), and the drawing should be derived from that metadata and from the algorithm's own structure. The final title remains the primary output; the metadata is optional and reused by whoever wants to render it. The rendering itself should stay simple enough that a non-technical user reads it at a glance.

## Why it matters

The title a card shows is the result of a three-way decision that depends on a board-level mode plus two per-card inputs, and today the only way to learn why a card is called what it is called is to read `cardTitle.ts`. A user who renames the file, sets an override, or edits a heading and sees no change has no feedback loop. Making the two inputs explicit fields and previewing the outcome closes that loop; explaining the decision from the algorithm's own trace keeps the explanation honest as the algorithm evolves.

## Notes for whoever picks this up

- The panel's `titleWithoutOverride` (`CardDetail.tsx`, around line 1066) already recomputes the fallback for the "Display title" placeholder by calling `resolveTitle` on the current body; the live preview generalises that to both fields.
- A file-name field in the panel means in-app renames become a routine panel action, so the remount-on-rename behaviour recorded in 20260826.09 becomes far more visible.
- The context-menu action "Display title" (`focusDisplayTitle`) and its label will need to follow the rename of the field.
