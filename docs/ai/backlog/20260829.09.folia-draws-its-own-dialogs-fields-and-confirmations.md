# Folia draws its own dialogs, its own field rows and its own confirmations, where the app has all three

## What is asked

The audit of 2026-08-29 ([the report](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/README.md)) found three layers of the same duplication: the dialog shell (finding 01-03, with 06-02 and 06-03 in [section 06](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/06.interaction-and-input.md)), the controls inside it (01-04), and the destructive confirmations (01-13, cross-referenced as 06-08). Obsidian provides `Modal`, `Setting` with its component builders, and `ConfirmationModal`; the findings name the members and the versions.

## Current behaviour

Two hand-built shells. `src/ui/ColumnEditModal.tsx:49` portals a `.folia-modal-backdrop` to the document body, wraps a `role="dialog" aria-modal="true"` div, and hand-writes the Escape handler, the focus-on-open, the header close button and the Cancel/Save footer. `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx` has three `role="dialog"` returns (`:1285`, `:1357`, `:1430`) with their own Escape handling and focus capture and restore, but is a dialog in only one of its three presentation modes.

Inside the first shell, nine controls are built as `<label className="folia-field">` pairs (`src/ui/ColumnEditModal.tsx:126-263`), including a bare `<input type="checkbox">` where the app's own switch would be, two `<input type="range">` sliders and two `<select>` dropdowns; the same pattern recurs in `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx` (`:1530`, `:1565`, `:1778`, `:1824`) and in four bare `<textarea>` elements. Meanwhile `src/main.ts:939-983` drives `addToggle`, `addDropdown`, `addSlider` and `addText` correctly, so the plugin already knows the API where it is allowed to use it.

Three destructive confirmations (`src/ui/CardItem.tsx:417`, `src/ui/ColumnMenu.tsx:220`, `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx:1495`) are `role="alertdialog"` blocks rendered in place, deliberately lighter than a whole-window dialog.

The costs are specific and recorded in the findings: `tracking/waivers/0002-automated-a11y-gate.md` notes that both hand-built dialogs declare a modal role without a real focus trap or an inert background, and adopting `Modal` hands that behaviour to the app's implementation rather than to a documented promise, so it needs testing rather than assuming. `scripts/check-portal-scope.mjs:30-32` recognises only two container shapes and errors on anything else, so a native `contentEl` mount would have to be taught to it. `ConfirmationModal` landed after `minAppVersion` 1.7.2 and needs the `requireApiVersion` gate `src/main.ts:902` already uses.

## How to observe

Open the edit-column dialog and compare its checkbox, its sliders and its dropdowns against the same controls in Obsidian's own settings tab, on the same theme. Then press Tab repeatedly with the dialog open and watch focus leave it for the board behind. Delete a card and note that the confirmation is in place rather than a dialog, which is the behaviour worth keeping or losing on purpose.

## Expected outcome

A settled answer for each of the three layers: whether the column dialog becomes a host dialog (the findings read this one as the closest thing to a clear win, and `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx` as the one to leave alone), whether the toggle, the sliders and the dropdowns stop being browser controls, and whether the three in-place confirmations stay in place. Whatever is decided, the focus-trap gap in waiver 0002 is either closed or restated as accepted, because it does not close itself.
