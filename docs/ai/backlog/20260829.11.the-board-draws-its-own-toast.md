# The board draws its own toast instead of using the app's notice

## What is asked

Finding 01-07 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 01](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/01.ui-components-and-primitives.md), cross-referenced as 06-09) found the board's transient messages rendered by Folia itself, where Obsidian's `Notice` is the facility.

## Current behaviour

`src/ui/App.tsx:143` holds `toast` state, `showToast` (`:153`) sets it with a `window.setTimeout` of 4000 ms for errors and 2200 ms otherwise, a cleanup effect clears it on unmount (`:161`), and `:928-937` renders a `role="status" aria-live="polite"` element with an icon and the text. It is styled from `--folia-*` tokens, sits in Folia's own z-index ladder, and appears inside the board rather than where every other message in the app appears.

The finding reads it as the cleanest port candidate in its section: every transient message already funnels through one function, so reaching `Notice` from the React tree is a single `CardRepository` method plus a no-op in the fake repository. It also notes what should not move: three in-place messages that belong to the surface they annotate rather than to a global notice.

## How to observe

Do anything on the board that reports success or failure (move a card into a full column, fail a rename) and watch where the message appears and how it is styled, then trigger any Obsidian message (a failed command, a sync notice) and compare position, styling and dismissal.

## Expected outcome

Transient board messages appear where the user already expects the app's messages to appear, or a written decision that a board-local toast is deliberate. The three in-place messages the finding names are explicitly out of scope of whichever answer is chosen.
