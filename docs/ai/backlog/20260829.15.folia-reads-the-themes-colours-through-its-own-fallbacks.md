# Folia reads the theme's colours through its own fallbacks and its own mixes, so a themed board is only half themed

## What is asked

Ten findings of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 02](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/02.styling-theme-and-design-language.md)) are one decision seen ten times: colour is taken from Obsidian, then bent. They are 02-02, 02-03, 02-04, 02-05, 02-06, 02-07, 02-08, 02-20, 02-24 and 02-31. Several are single-declaration corrections; they are grouped here because they are one pass over one file, not ten separate jobs.

## Current behaviour

The individual shapes, each with its locations in the finding:

- Every theme colour is read through a hard-coded hex fallback that can only ever be wrong — `var(--color-red, #e5534b)` and five more, across nine declarations (02-03), plus ten `var(--text-faint, var(--text-muted))` fallbacks for a variable Obsidian always defines (02-05). None of the nine is seen by the raw-value audit, because they sit on `--folia-*` declaration lines that `scripts/audit-raw-values.mjs:74` exempts wholesale.
- The eight column colours are fixed hexes (`src/ui/columnColors.ts:3-12`) where Obsidian ships eight theme-aware ones; these are also written into users' board notes, so changing them needs a stored form and a migration rule (02-02).
- Status, active and selected states are rebuilt from the palette by hand where semantic variables exist for them (02-04, 02-07), form fields are painted with the page background instead of the form-field background (02-08), icon buttons hard-code their rest and hover colours beside the icon colour variables (02-20), accent *text* is painted with the interactive-element accent rather than the text accent (02-06), completed items ignore the checklist-done variables and carry a workaround comment because of it (02-24), and links use one link variable out of fifteen while unresolved links use none (02-31).

## How to observe

Install a theme that restyles Obsidian's colour variables, open a board beside a normal note, and read the two together: the inputs, the icon buttons, the links, the completed items and the "on" states are the places where the board stops following.

## Expected outcome

Colour in the board comes from the user's theme wherever the theme has an opinion, with the exceptions named on purpose — the finding is explicit that some of these are deliberate (the accent-tinted filter chip, the tuned contrast on the priority chips) and that the column palette is a product decision that has to be made before it can be migrated.
