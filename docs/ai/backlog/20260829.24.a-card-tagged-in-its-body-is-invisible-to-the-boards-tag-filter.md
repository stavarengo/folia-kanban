# A card tagged in its body is invisible to the board's own tag filter

## What happens

Finding 04-06 of the audit of 2026-08-29 ([section 04](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/04.vault-files-and-metadata.md)).

`tagValues` (`src/ui/cardView.ts:269`) reads a card's tags from `frontmatter.area` and `frontmatter["tags"]` only, and feeds that to the board's `tag:` filter term and to the free-text haystack (`:416`). Obsidian treats a tag written anywhere in a note's body as a tag of that note, and exposes both kinds together through `getAllTags`. So a card tagged the ordinary Obsidian way, in its text, does not match the board's tag filter, and nothing on screen says why: the card is simply not in the result.

The fix location is not where the bug reads: `src/ui/cardView.ts` cannot import `obsidian`, so the tags have to be resolved in the adapter during `loadBoard` and arrive on the card.

## How to reproduce

Take a card note and write a tag in its body rather than in its frontmatter. On the board, filter by that tag: the card is missing. Search the same term as free text and it is found, which is what makes the tag case read as a bug rather than a design.

## Expected outcome

The board's idea of a card's tags matches Obsidian's, or the board says plainly that it filters on the `tags` property only.
