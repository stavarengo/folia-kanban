# The card menu and the column popover are a second menu implementation, themed and keyboarded by Folia alone

## What is asked

The audit of 2026-08-29 ([the report](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/README.md), findings 01-01, 01-02, 01-14, and the cross-references 06-01 and 06-13 in [section 06](../reports/20260829.what-obsidian-already-provides/06.interaction-and-input.md)) found that the two surfaces the board opens on a right-click or a column header are built entirely by hand, where Obsidian ships `Menu` and `MenuItem` (see finding 01-01 for the API members and the developer-docs page that documents this exact shape).

## Current behaviour

`src/ui/CardContextMenu.tsx:39` is a roughly 235-line component that portals a `role="menu"` div to the document body, measures and clamps itself against `window.innerWidth`/`innerHeight` with hard-coded 8px gutters (`:70-71`, and the same pattern again in `src/ui/ColumnMenu.tsx:55` and `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx:1209` — finding 01-14), closes itself on a document `pointerdown`, runs its own arrow-key roving focus, restores focus to the opener, and draws dividers, section labels, a danger item and two radio grids itself. `src/ui/ColumnMenu.tsx:33` is a `role="dialog"` popover with six action rows plus a rename input, a colour swatch grid and a WIP-limit field, positioned against a hard-coded `MENU_W = 224`.

Two consequences are already written down elsewhere in the repository: `tracking/waivers/0002-automated-a11y-gate.md` holds two open behavioural gaps that are this code (the card menu is pointer-only, and its arrow keys do not reach the priority radio group), and `scripts/check-portal-scope.mjs` plus the `jsx-a11y` waivers in `eslint.config.mjs` exist to police shapes a native menu would not have.

Not a mechanical swap, and the findings say why. The priority and column rows are grids of coloured chips, several per line; as `MenuItem.setChecked` rows they become long flat lists and lose the colour coding. The column popover's three fields have no `Menu` equivalent at all, and they duplicate `src/ui/ColumnEditModal.tsx` (`:128`, `:142`, `:170`), which the popover's own "Edit column…" row opens. `src/ui/**` may not import `obsidian`, so reaching any native menu means a `CardRepository` port method plus a `FakeRepo` no-op, the way `src/obsidian/propertySuggest.ts` already does it.

## How to observe

Right-click a card on the board and try to reach the menu from the keyboard alone, then open it and press the arrow keys through to the priority chips. Open a column's header menu and compare its rename and colour controls against the edit-column dialog behind its own "Edit column…" row. On a phone, the app's own menus are a bottom sheet (`.is-phone .menu` in Obsidian's 1.13.7 stylesheet); Folia's are not.

## Expected outcome

One decision, written down, about what these two surfaces are: whether the action rows become the app's own menu and what happens to the chip grids and the popover's fields, or whether Folia keeps its own implementation knowingly, with the keyboard and phone gaps it currently carries named as accepted. Either answer settles what the next menu-shaped surface in the plugin should be, and lets the two open a11y waiver items be closed or restated.
