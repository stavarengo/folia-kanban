# No way to filter or search the board down to cards with unread comments

## What happens

Cards now carry an unread-comments state: the tile's comment badge turns blue (unread) or purple (a reply to one of your own), and the detail panel marks the new comments under a "New" rule. That state is readable one tile at a time, but there is no way to ask the board "show me only the cards with something I haven't read".

The search grammar in `src/ui/cardView.ts` understands `area:`, `status:`, `priority:`, `tag:`, `due:` and `context:` (`FilterKey` / `FILTER_KEYS`), all of which answer from the `Card` object alone plus a small `MatchContext` (`{ today, doneColumnId }`). Unread state cannot: it is derived from `settings.userName` + `settings.commentsSeen` (plugin data, keyed by card path) combined with the card's `stats.commentMarks`. So an `unread:` token would need `MatchContext` widened and fed at both of its construction sites — `Column.tsx` (~line 210) and `App.tsx` (~line 594, the toolbar's match counter) — plus a `Toolbar.tsx` suggestion entry and tests.

The same grammar backs a column's `filter:` property, which is the part that makes this more than plumbing: a column filter is written into the **board note's frontmatter** and travels with the vault, while `commentsSeen` is per install and never leaves the machine. A board note saying `filter: "unread:comments"` would therefore mean something different for every person and every machine that opens it — a column that is full for one reader and empty for another, with nothing in the note explaining why.

## Why it matters

On a board with many cards, an unread badge you have to hunt for tile by tile does much less than a one-key filter would — finding what agents and other people left for you is the whole point of the feature. The chips and `/` search are the board's established way of narrowing to "what needs me right now" (`due:overdue`, `due:soon`), and unread comments belong to that same question.

## Expected outcome

Some way to narrow the board to cards with unread comments (and ideally, separately, to the ones that read as replies to your own comments), consistent with how the existing quick filters and the `/` search behave.

Whatever shape it takes has to answer the question above first: whether a vault-local, per-install notion of "unread" may appear in a token that a board note can persist as a column filter, or whether it belongs only to the transient search box and the toolbar chips.

## Likely code areas

- `src/ui/cardView.ts` — `FilterKey`, `FILTER_KEYS`, `MatchContext`, `matchToken`
- `src/ui/Column.tsx`, `src/ui/App.tsx` — the two places `MatchContext` is built
- `src/ui/Toolbar.tsx` — token suggestions and the quick-filter chips
- `src/model/unread.ts` — `unreadComments`, the pure predicate any filter would call
