# The detail panel's section inputs follow you to the next card

## What happens

`App.tsx` renders one `<CardDetail>` instance for every card (no `key`), so navigating from card A to card B keeps the component and all its state. The panel's per-card state is reset by hand on a path change (`postedHere`, `seenOnOpen`, the description draft, and the property fields keyed by path), but the five section inputs are not: `newComment`, `newTodo`, `newSubcard`, the relationship link inputs and the add-property form in `src/ui/CardDetail.tsx` keep whatever was half-typed. Type half a comment on card A, click card B, press Enter, and it posts to B.

Two smaller symptoms share the cause. `readSeq` in `reload()` is bumped by every reload, including one started for a card since navigated away from, so B's in-flight read can be discarded as "older" and the panel shows a blank body until the next board reload (it self-heals, but the guard does not do what its comment says). And the one-shot focus flags (`focusAddSubcard`, `focusDisplayTitle`) never re-fire when the same action is invoked again on an already-open card, because `openCard` clears the flag and the action sets it in the same batch.

## How to reproduce

Open a card, type into "Write a comment" without pressing Enter, click another card's tile, press Enter in the comment box. The comment lands on the second card.

## Why it matters

This is the pre-existing half of a rule the panel now states and enforces for its property fields ("what was typed on one card must not be sitting in the field when the panel moves to the next one", `CardDetail.tsx`, next to the `PriorityField`). Half the panel follows the rule and half does not, and the half that does not is where a wrong write goes to the wrong note.

## Expected outcome

Moving the panel to another card either drops the half-typed section inputs or keeps them visibly attached to the card they were typed on; either way, Enter never writes A's text into B. Keying the panel by path in `App.tsx` would settle all three symptoms at once, but the file's comments document a deliberate reliance on instance reuse (the add-card flows change the path of the mounted panel), so that is a decision, not a one-liner.
